What is plywood?

Plywood, a composite material, is manufactured from plies or thin layers of wood veneer glued together in adjacent layers. It is an engineered wood from the manufactured board family, including Oriented Strand Board (OSB), Medium-Density Fibreboard (MDF) and particle board.

Simply put, plywood is a solid composite material made from thin veneer of wood peeled from logs. These layers are sandwiched with binders, pressed and then heated to secure. The plies are laid at various angles so that the grains go one way on the first layer and the other way on further layers.

While wood expands and contracts after contact with moisture, the alternating layers of plywood suppress that movement, making it dimensionally stable. The materials and composition make it resistant to cracking, twisting, breaking, bending and shrinking.

Different Types of Plywood & Their Uses

Softwood Plywood

Made from cedar, redwood or pinewood. Commonly used for roof covering, home flooring, frame sheathing, sheds, shelves and dog houses.

₹50 – ₹175 / sq.ft Strong but lightweight

Hardwood Plywood

Sturdy, made of 7+ layers from oak, maple, walnut and birch. Used in furniture, musical instruments, sports equipment and durable wood casings.

₹55 – ₹297 / sq.ft Highly durable

Aircraft Plywood

High-grade, moisture and heat resistant. Made of mahogany and birch. Used in aircraft, boats, rafters, heavy-load furniture and industrial buildings.

₹200 – ₹400+ (Premium) / sq.ft Top grade durability

Lumber Core Plywood

3-ply design with thick wood core and hardwood outer veneers. Soft interior makes nailing and screwing easy. Ideal for home interior work.

₹70 – ₹265 / sq.ft Highly durable

Exterior Plywood

Weather and water-resistant adhesive. Also called multi-ply. Mould and mildew resistant even in moist locations. Used for exterior woodwork.

₹65 – ₹150 / sq.ft Durable

Marine Plywood

Highest grade construction plywood with waterproof adhesives. No knotholes. Made from larch or firewood. Not chemically treated, so not rot-resistant.

₹110 – ₹300 / sq.ft Durable but not rot-resistant

Flexible Plywood

Ideal for curves and bends. Used for curved construction frames, fancy cabinets and furniture. Cross-grained centre with thicker outer plies.

₹150 – ₹750+ (Premium) / sq.ft Not durable

Sanded Plywood

Finely sanded top and bottom layers for smooth surface. Used for visible areas: panels, shelves, bookshelves. 3+ cross-laminated layers.

₹55 – ₹195 / sq.ft Durable

Advantages of Plywood

  • Better strength and durability compared to MDF.
  • Available in thicknesses from 6mm to 32mm.
  • Better water resistance.
  • Made from different wood species.
  • Easily painted or polished.
  • Can hold screws and nails.
  • Offers a smoother surface.
  • Tailored to different sizes and shapes.
  • Resistant to warping, twisting and cracking.
  • More budget-friendly than solid wood.
  • Available in large sizes for seamless interiors.

Disadvantages of Plywood

  • Softer than solid wood or metal; may not withstand heavy items.
  • Can chip and shatter when cut due to layered structure.
  • May be more expensive than other engineered wood.
  • Extra edge treatment needed for better appearance.
  • Lower resale value compared to solid wood furniture.
  • Can warp and bend if it absorbs moisture.
  • May release small amounts of formaldehyde.
  • Possible manufacturing defects like delamination.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does plywood need fumigation?
Fumigation is a pesticide treatment that kills pests inside wood. It is necessary to fumigate plywood to ensure its longevity.
Which plywood is best for furniture?
Any A-grade plywood is the best choice for furniture. You can also go for aircraft or hardwood plywoods.
Which plywood is best for a modular kitchen?
Some of the best kinds for Indian modular kitchens are marine, moisture-resistant, and boiling water-resistant plywoods.
Can plywood be recycled?
Yes, it falls in Class B of recycling. It needs to be shredded, purified, and turned into mulch for landscaping or animal bedding.
Can plywood be curved?
Yes, for curved surfaces, opt for flexible plywood as it can easily bend without breaking.
How long does plywood furniture last?
Plywood has high durability compared to other wood types. It can survive up to 35 years since it is manufactured from natural plantation wood.
